# Thock+ v1.0.0 — Initial Release 🔊 ⚡

The all-in-one mechanical sound and hardware haptic companion for macOS.

### ✨ What's New
- **⌨️ Mechanical Keyboard Audio**: High-fidelity mechanical switch audio on key-down and key-release (built-in Alps SKCM Blue & Cherry MX switch profiles).
- **🖱️ Micro-Switch Mouse Audio**: Crisp mechanical switch sounds on press and release for left, right, and middle clicks (Razer Orochi V2 profile).
- **🔄 Mechanical Scroll Detents**: All 7 hand-tuned mechanical detent voices (*Nok*, *Vyl*, *Glu*, *Dib*, *Plo*, *Tok*, *Rub*).
- **⚡ Direct `MTActuator` Hardware Haptics**: Direct IOKit connection to Apple Taptic Engine solenoids with zero WindowServer drop and 5 tactile feelings (*Wheel Ratchet*, *Crisp Detent*, *Light Tick*, *Medium Click*, *Firm Thock*).
- **🛑 Cadence Limiter**: Real-time rate limiting so fast flings stay locked to a clean, rhythmic cadence without sound spamming.
- **🎵 Soundpacks Manager**: Live browser & 1-click downloader for 23+ community switch soundpacks from the official registry, plus custom folder drag-and-drop.
- **🖥️ Full macOS Settings Window & Menu Bar Extra**: Native SwiftUI + AppKit interface with volume sliders, detent density tuning, and instant interactive test pad.
